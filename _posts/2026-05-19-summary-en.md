Companies like OpenAI and Anthropic are actively developing frameworks (like the Agents SDK) to facilitate this, making robust API tooling strategically important.

This trend indicates a significant contraction in job prospects for CS graduates, a field traditionally viewed as a pathway to high employment and salary, which could reshape student career planning and university program strategies. Specific data points include Princeton's CS major headcount dropping from 150 to 74, and UC Berkeley's EECS graduates expected to fall from 1029 to about 350 (a ~59% decline). The hiring contraction extends beyond tech, with JPMorgan Chase's intern acceptance rate falling to 0.7% and PwC planning to cut entry-level hires by a third.
