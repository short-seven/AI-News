## [AI-assisted coding: slower pace yields higher quality code.](https://nolanlawson.com/2026/05/25/using-ai-to-write-better-code-more-slowly/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

The analysis argues that while AI code generation tools like LLMs can produce higher-quality code, the practical workflow often results in a slower overall development process due to the necessary iterative cycles of generation, review, and refinement. This insight is significant as it challenges the common assumption that AI tools primarily boost development speed, instead highlighting a critical trade-off between code quality and velocity that developers and organizations must consider when integrating AI into their workflows. The workflow involves a long back-and-forth process, potentially using multiple specialized AI models (e.g., one for initial code generation, another for review) to catch edge cases and improve robustness, even if it takes more time than writing the code manually.

hackernews · signa11 · May 25, 23:16

**Background**: Large Language Models (LLMs) for code generation are AI systems trained on vast datasets of code, enabling them to generate code snippets from natural language prompts. While they excel at producing syntactically correct and often logical initial code, they can still make subtle errors or miss edge cases, necessitating human or AI-assisted review. The process of iterative refinement, where code is generated, reviewed, and revised in cycles, is a common technique to improve output quality.

## [EU Preliminary Investigation Finds Google May Have Violated Digital Markets Act](https://t.me/zaihuapd/41566) ⭐️ 7.0/10

The EU Commission's preliminary investigation suggests Alphabet has violated the Digital Markets Act by engaging in self-preferencing behaviors in Google Search and the Play Store. This investigation could lead to significant fines and force major changes in how Google operates its core services in the EU, setting a precedent for regulating other large tech platforms under the new DMA framework. The investigation specifically points to Google prioritizing its own shopping, flight, and hotel services in search results, and restricting app developers in the Play Store from directing users to alternative purchasing channels.

telegram · zaihuapd · May 26, 00:27

**Background**: The Digital Markets Act (DMA) is an EU competition law that came into full effect in March 2024, designed to prevent large online platforms, designated as 'gatekeepers', from abusing their market power. A key provision of the DMA prohibits these gatekeepers from engaging in self-preferencing, which is the practice of favoring their own products or services over those of competitors on their platforms.
